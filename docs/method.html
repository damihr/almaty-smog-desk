How it works

From a sensor reading to a timetable decision

PE

Who it's for: the PE teacher or duty officer at an Almaty school

Almaty sits in a bowl under the Zailiysky Alatau. From October to April, coal smoke from private-housing districts gets trapped under cold-air inversions. Children breathe faster than adults and count as a sensitive group. The teacher decides the afternoon before, or first thing in the morning, whether PE and recess go outdoors. This tool gives a number and a verdict at that moment.

Pipeline

Real measurements

Daily PM2.5 from OpenAQ: the US Embassy reference monitor (Apr 2020 – Nov 2025) and the School No.182 low-cost sensor (Dec 2025 – now). We keep only days with at least 18 of 24 hours measured.

Weather

Open-Meteo ERA5 archive for training, and the Open-Meteo forecast for live use: temperature, minimum temperature, humidity, wind, rain/snow, pressure and cloud, for today and tomorrow.

Features known on the day

Today's and yesterday's PM2.5, the 7-day mean, the trend, today's weather and tomorrow's forecast, season, heating-season flag and weekend.

Split by date, never shuffled

Train ≤ Sep 2024 · validation season Oct 2023 – Sep 2024 (used only to choose the warning cut-off) · test Oct 2024 – Nov 2025 · second test at School No.182.

Model and decision

Ridge regression beat gradient boosting and the persistence baseline. Warning: INDOORS if the forecast is above the cut-off, OUTDOORS-LIGHT between 15 and the cut-off, OUTDOORS below 15.

What moves the forecast

Ridge weights on standardised inputs. A positive weight means the input raises tomorrow's forecast.

Honest limits

One reference station

The embassy monitor stopped reporting in Nov 2025, so the test period ends there. The school sensor is a low-cost device and is less accurate than a reference monitor.

Weather in training is reanalysis

Training uses what the weather actually was, but live use relies on a forecast. Real errors will be somewhat larger.

Sudden events

Snow fronts, wind and fireworks cause the biggest misses (see Results).

Not medical advice

This project is a prototype and is not medical advice. Schools should follow official guidance from Kazhydromet and health authorities.
