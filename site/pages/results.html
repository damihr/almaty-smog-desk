Forecast error

Mean absolute error in µg/m³ (lower is better). The baseline is "tomorrow will be the same as today", which is hard to beat for air quality.

The school decision

A smog day is a daily mean above 35.4 µg/m³ (US EPA: unhealthy for sensitive groups, which includes children). We count a missed smog day as 3× worse than a false alarm and chose the warning cut-off on a validation season (Oct 2023 – Sep 2024), never on the test data.

Second test: a sensor the model never saw

Where it fails

The biggest misses on the embassy test set fall into two patterns. Extreme spikes (above 100 µg/m³, on still, freezing days) are under-forecast by about 50, because a linear model pulls extremes toward the average. It still says INDOORS on those days, so the decision is right even when the number is too low. Sudden clean-ups (15 Jan 2025: 90 → 23) happen when an inversion breaks before the change is visible in the daily weather.
