How wrong is the free air-quality data?

Most weather and air apps show CAMS model estimates, not measurements. We compared CAMS with the US Embassy reference monitor for every day both had data.

Average PM2.5 by month

The model estimate is close in summer but far too low in winter, exactly when it matters.

Why it matters.CAMS works on a grid of about 11 km, so it averages Almaty's polluted lower districts with clean mountain air, and it can't see an inversion trapping smoke under 100 m of cold air. A school relying on a free app would get a "fine" signal on many real smog days. That's why our final model is trained and tested on real station measurements.
